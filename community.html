Our Community

Be part of positive change in local communities

Tanzania is a diverse country with vibrant communities, cultures and traditions. While the country continues to experience economic and social development, some communities still face challenges in areas such as education, healthcare, employment, infrastructure and access to essential services.

Community volunteers can contribute their time, skills and ideas while working alongside local organizations and community members. The goal is not to replace local expertise, but to support existing initiatives and contribute to sustainable, community-led solutions.

In rural communities, agriculture remains an important part of everyday life, while access to education, healthcare, clean water and sanitation can remain challenging. In urban areas such as Mwanza, communities may face challenges including youth unemployment, limited economic opportunities and unequal access to services.

Through our community programs, volunteers can gain a deeper understanding of life in Tanzania while contributing to initiatives that support education, health, livelihoods, inclusion, environmental sustainability and community development.

How Can You Help?

Ways volunteers can make a difference

Volunteers support local organizations and community initiatives by sharing their skills, knowledge, time and experience. Depending on your interests and qualifications, you may contribute to education, healthcare, economic empowerment, agriculture, youth development, community development or conservation.

Education and Teaching

Support schools and learning initiatives by assisting with English, mathematics, science and other educational activities. Volunteers may also support reading programs, educational activities and the collection or organization of learning materials for schools.

Healthcare and Health Education

Qualified volunteers can support local healthcare initiatives within their professional scope. Other volunteers may assist with community health education, hygiene awareness, sanitation initiatives and health promotion activities.

Women and Gender Equality

Support initiatives that promote women's economic participation, financial literacy, entrepreneurship and access to opportunities. Volunteers may also contribute to awareness activities and community discussions focused on equality and inclusion.

Agriculture and Sustainable Development

Support local agricultural initiatives and sustainability projects. Depending on the placement, volunteers may contribute to sustainable farming education, environmental awareness, clean-water initiatives and sanitation projects.

Youth Empowerment and Skills Development

Help young people develop practical skills through mentorship, life-skills activities, employability training and entrepreneurship initiatives. Volunteers can share knowledge that helps young people explore education, employment and small-business opportunities.

Community Development

Contribute to community-led development initiatives that improve local environments and access to essential services. Activities may include community facilities, sanitation, environmental improvement and other approved development projects.

Wildlife and Environmental Conservation

Participate in environmental awareness and conservation initiatives that promote responsible tourism and sustainable communities. Activities may include sustainability campaigns, environmental education and community conservation efforts.

Community Partnerships

Organizations and initiatives you may work with

We work with local organizations and community initiatives to connect volunteers with meaningful placements based on their interests, skills and availability.

Tanzania Early Childhood Education & Care (TECEC)

Tanzania Early Childhood Education & Care (TECEC) works to support early childhood development by empowering parents, caregivers and preschool teachers with resources and knowledge that promote children's health, development and learning.

Located in Nyegezi, Mwanza, TECEC provides an opportunity for volunteers interested in early childhood education and community development.

Visit TECEC Website

Beacon Teens Challenge

Beacon Teens Challenge is a community-focused initiative supporting young people and individuals affected by social challenges. Volunteers may have opportunities to support educational, awareness and community activities, depending on the needs of the organization.

Ask About This Placement

Upendo Daima (UDO)

Upendo Daima, meaning "Unconditional Love", is a community initiative associated with supporting vulnerable children in Mwanza. Its work has included support for children's education, healthcare, basic needs and family reintegration.

Learn More About UDO

WoteSawa

WoteSawa is a non-governmental organization based in Mwanza that advocates for the rights of domestic workers and works to combat human trafficking, including child trafficking. Its work involves domestic workers, employers, families and community members.

Visit WoteSawa Website

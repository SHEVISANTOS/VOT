A Little Information About Us

A trusted local organization creating meaningful experiences

Volunteers of Tanzania (VOT-Mwanza) came to life in 2018, the year we were legally registered as an organization that assists interns, students, and volunteers who want to come to Africa and find suitable placements to help the community.

This initiative is driven by our passion to transform communities and alleviate poverty and life hardships in Africa.

Since 2018, we have been hosting volunteers and interns from different countries, and from this initiative we are witnessing the changes in our communities.

To ensure interns and volunteers are well accommodated during their stay, the company also provides accommodation and transport services for the entire duration of the volunteer experience.

Our Mission

VOT Mwanza's mission is focused on encouraging all people around the world to volunteer in developing countries. Volunteering will help the world community to better understand the needs and challenges faced by developing countries. In the long run, communities around the world will become more aware and give necessary assistance related to problems in developing countries.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see the life of underdeveloped communities improved through the organization of internships and volunteering programs. Through internships, students' practical training, and voluntary works, we believe a multinational community is created.

Discover Your Host City

Mwanza City, also known as Rock City to the residents, is a port city and capital of Mwanza Region on the southern shore of Lake Victoria in north-western Tanzania. With an urban population of over 3.6 million, it is Tanzania's second-largest city. The Sukuma constitute over 90% of the region's population, alongside smaller communities including the Zinza, Haya, Sumbwa, Nyamwezi, Luo, Kurya, Jita, Shashi, and Kerewe.

🗣️ Language

Swahili is Tanzania’s official language, and English is used alongside it. Most people speak their tribal language and Swahili, with many speaking English as a third language. Learning a few basic Swahili words will go a long way Tanzanians will love to interact with you!

🤝 Greetings

Greetings are an important part of Tanzanian culture. Handshakes may last longer than you expect this is normal. Tanzanians treat the elderly with the greatest respect. To greet an elder, say "Shikamoo", and they will respond "Marahaba".

⛪ Religion

Christianity is the most practiced religion (~63%), followed by Islam (~34%). Around 1.2% follow traditional religions, and 1.8% are non-believers. Tanzania is known for its peaceful coexistence among faiths.

👗 Dress Code

Tanzanians generally dress conservatively. Women traditionally wear long skirts with knees and shoulders covered. In places like Zanzibar, there is a strict dress code. At the coast, you'll discover the colourful kikoi (sarong for men) and kanga (sarong for women).

Accommodation

Feel at Home

During your stay in Tanzania, you'll be accommodated at VOT Hostel in Nyegezi, just 8 km from Mwanza City Center and 3 km from St. Augustine University of Tanzania (SAUT).

Our hostel is located on the roadside, making it easy to access public transport if needed. Our main house can host up to 20 guests at once. Don't worry; if you have a team of more than 20 people, we will always have a solution for that. You won't have to worry about companionship, as there are always volunteers living in the house.

Our local staff will prepare breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily, taking special dietary needs into account. Meals include a mix of traditional African dishes and some Western cuisine. Complimentary tea, milk, coffee, and drinking water are provided while at VOT House.

If you enjoy cooking, you are always welcome in the kitchen. Soft drinks and beer are available for purchase in nearby stores.

Bedrooms

Bedrooms are designed to provide comfort throughout your stay. For those seeking a tranquil escape, we also offer vibrant shared bedrooms that foster a sense of community among travelers. The choice is entirely yours, allowing you to select the option that best suits your desires and needs.

No matter which you choose a private or shared bedroom our dedicated team is committed to ensuring that your experience with us is enjoyable and memorable.

We have both shared bedrooms with shared washrooms and self contained bedrooms fitted with furniture to allow you to engage in personal study and other table activities. Don't worry about towels and bed sheets; we've got you covered!

Main House & Facilities

The main house features a spacious sitting room, Western-style toilets, showers with hot water, and a kitchen. Each bedroom is equipped with bed sheets, towels, mosquito nets, and either air conditioning or a fan. Depending on your privacy needs, the bedrooms in the main house can be used privately, but this can be for an additional cost.

Free Wi-Fi is also provided. However, it is important to note that there are often power outages, which may affect the availability of hot water.

Enjoy the fenced yard, green garden, swimming pool and flowers surrounding the house for relaxation with friends. The place is very safe and for your safety at night, we have a night guard who is always on duty.

Shared bedrooms house

Our other house has a self contained room and other shared rooms with shared washrooms. Each shared room has two bunk beds to accommodate up to 4 people. They also have tables for extra activities in the room.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does volunteering cost? +

At VOT, we don't charge for volunteering or placement; the fee is for your daily expenses during your entire stay in Mwanza. Visit our Pricing page for full details.

Where will I stay? +

You will stay at VOT Hostel in Nyegezi, just 8 km from Mwanza City Centre. The hostel features private and shared rooms, a garden, swimming pool, hot showers, and free Wi-Fi.

Are meals included? +

Yes — breakfast, lunch, and dinner are prepared daily by our local staff. Special dietary needs are accommodated. Tea, coffee, and drinking water are complimentary.

Do I need previous experience? +

Most programs welcome volunteers regardless of experience level. For healthcare placements, some medical background is preferred. We match you with a placement that fits your skills.

How long can I volunteer? +

We accept volunteers for as little as 1 week up to 6 months or more. The minimum recommended stay for meaningful impact is 2–4 weeks.

How do I get from the airport to Mwanza? +

Airport pickup is included. Simply share your flight details with us after applying and our team will meet you at Mwanza Airport (MWZ).

Is Wi-Fi available? +

Yes, free Wi-Fi is provided at the hostel and its available in all common areas around the property.

What happens after I apply? +

Our team contacts you within 2–3 business days to confirm your placement, discuss dates and fees, and answer any remaining questions. We then send a full pre-departure information pack.

What is your child safeguarding policy? +

Volunteers placed in our Education and Community programs work with children, so every volunteer is briefed on our child protection code of conduct before starting. This covers safe, appropriate interaction with children at all times, a strict no-photography-without-consent rule, and a direct line to our local staff to report any concern. Our permanent local team is present at every placement site throughout your stay.

What is your cancellation and refund policy? +

If you need to cancel before your placement starts, contact us as early as possible — the more notice you give, the more of your fee we can refund, since it covers accommodation and placement arrangements made on your behalf. If you need to cut your stay short after arriving, tell your local coordinator; unused nights of accommodation and meals from that point are refunded, though placement and arrival costs already committed are not. Contact us directly for the exact terms that apply to your dates.

Can I volunteer alone? +

Yes — most of our volunteers travel solo. You'll live at VOT Hostel alongside other volunteers from around the world and a full-time local team, so you're never really on your own, and lasting friendships form fast.

What are the daily working hours? +

Most placements run Monday to Friday, mornings into early afternoon. Healthcare placements can offer longer or shift-based hours if you want more hands-on time. We confirm your exact schedule with the placement before you arrive.

Do I need travel insurance? +

Yes — we require all volunteers to arrive with comprehensive travel and medical insurance, including cover for medical evacuation. VOT does not provide insurance, so please arrange your own before you travel and bring proof of cover.

Is there an age requirement? +

Most volunteers are between 18 and 50, but we welcome all ages. Volunteers under 18 can join with a parent or guardian, and there's no upper age limit — get in touch and we'll help find the right fit.

What about malaria and mosquito protection? +

Mwanza sits on Lake Victoria in a malaria risk area, so we recommend seeing a doctor or travel clinic about antimalarial medication before you fly. Every bed at VOT Hostel is fitted with a mosquito net, and we advise using repellent and covering up in the evenings.

What currency should I bring? +

The local currency is the Tanzanian Shilling (TZS). US dollars in cash (2009 or newer bills) are widely accepted for larger payments such as visa fees, and ATMs are available in Mwanza city centre for withdrawing shillings. We recommend telling your bank about your travel dates in advance.

Can I combine volunteering with a safari or Zanzibar trip? +

Absolutely — many volunteers add on a safari, a Zanzibar beach trip, or local Mwanza activities before or after their placement. Let us know your interest and our team can help you plan it.

Is it safe to volunteer in Mwanza? +

Yes. You're met at the airport by VOT staff, housed in our supervised hostel, and given a full safety and city orientation in your first days. A staff member is reachable 24/7 for the length of your stay.

What isn't included in the weekly price? +

Your international flights, travel insurance, visa or residence permit, and personal spending money. Everything related to housing, meals, transport and placement support is included.

Do I need to speak Swahili? +

No. Most placements operate comfortably in English, and every volunteer receives a 5-day Swahili crash course in their first week to help with day-to-day life.

Can my group or university apply together? +

Yes. Groups of 5–7 receive a 5% per-person discount, and groups of 8 or more receive 10% off. Contact us directly to arrange group placements and dates.

About this program

Recognized as one of the Seven New Wonders of the World, Serengeti National Park is globally renowned for the spectacular wildebeest and zebra migration. It's just a 2-hour drive from Mwanza, which makes it perfect for volunteers who are short on time but still want a real taste of the Big Four and, in season, the great migration — whether as a single day trip or as the start of a longer safari.

  • The Big Four: lion, leopard, elephant and buffalo
  • Over 500 bird species unique to the region
  • Cheetahs, hyenas and various reptiles
  • Great Migration (May–early June): over 2 million wildebeest and zebra on the move
  • 4WD safari vehicle with an expert guide, plus packed breakfast, lunch and beverages
  • Just a 2-hour drive from Mwanza — easily done as a day trip or extended into a longer safari

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need any experience for a safari day trip? +

No. Our driver-guides handle everything from park permits to game viewing — it suits volunteers on any placement, whatever your background or length of stay.

What should I bring? +

Sun protection, a hat, a camera or binoculars, a light layer for early starts, and a reusable water bottle. Packed breakfast, lunch and beverages are arranged for you.

Can I extend this into a longer safari? +

Yes — the same route into the Serengeti can be extended into a 2-3 day or 5-7 day Northern Circuit safari with Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire and Lake Manyara. Just tell us when you book.

About this program

This is a flexible, good-energy placement suited to gap-year volunteers and education students who enjoy working with young children. Days are built around play, feeding support at mealtimes, and a homework club for older community children — all run alongside the daycare's own Tanzanian staff, who lead every session and are always present with you.

  • Structured play and early-learning activities
  • Feeding support at meal times
  • Homework club for school-age children
  • Always paired with trained local caregivers — never alone with children
  • Good fit for gap-year, childcare and education students

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need childcare experience? +

No formal childcare qualification is required. Most programs welcome volunteers regardless of experience level — we match you with a placement that fits your skills and a genuine enjoyment of working with children.

What is your child safeguarding policy? +

Every volunteer on this program is briefed on our child protection code of conduct before starting. This covers safe, appropriate interaction with children at all times, a strict no-photography-without-consent rule, and a direct line to our local staff to report any concern. Our permanent local team is present at the placement site throughout your stay.

How long can I volunteer? +

We accept volunteers for as little as 1 week up to 24 weeks (about 6 months). The minimum recommended stay for meaningful impact on this program is 2–4 weeks.

Tanzania Safaris & Tours for Volunteers

Combine volunteering with adventure. VOT organizes safaris to Tanzania's national parks and Zanzibar Island for volunteers and interns.

Adventure, Organized For You

More than 15 parks and reserves to explore

We organize safaris to all tourism safari destinations in the country, including the famous Zanzibar Island. There are more than 15 different national parks and reservation areas in Tanzania — Tanzanian nature offers many memorable experiences.

  • Transport, guides, and entry fees arranged for you
  • Trips planned around your volunteer placement
  • Group and private safari options
  • Local expert drivers and guides

01 — Day Trips

Day Trips from Mwanza

Explore Mwanza and its surroundings on a guided day trip — a great add-on for volunteers with a free day during their placement.

Visit Bismarck Rock, the shores of Lake Victoria, Saanane Island — the country's only island national park — and lively local markets.

  • Bismarck Rock
  • Saanane Island
  • Lake Victoria shores
  • Local markets

Tanzania National Park Safaris

Book a safari to Serengeti, Ngorongoro, or other Tanzanian national parks during your volunteer placement with VOT.

03 — Island Getaway

Zanzibar Island Getaways

Extend your Tanzania volunteer trip with a Zanzibar Island getaway — beaches, culture, and history organized through VOT.

Wander Stone Town's alleys, relax on Nungwi's white beaches, and join a spice tour or sunset dhow cruise.

  • Stone Town
  • Nungwi Beach
  • Spice tours
  • Sunset dhow cruise

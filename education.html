Teaching & Education

Volunteer as a teaching assistant in Tanzanian schools with VOT. Support literacy, classroom learning, and youth education in Mwanza communities.

Apply Now All Programs

About this program

As a teaching assistant, you will support local teachers and bring energy to the classroom. No teaching experience is required — your enthusiasm and English practice are already valuable to students.

  • Assist teachers in local primary schools
  • Support literacy and English conversation practice
  • Help with lesson preparation and class activities
  • Mentor and encourage students

Location

Mwanza, Tanzania

Duration

Flexible — 1 to 12 weeks

Accommodation

VOT hostel in Nyegezi

Support

24/7 local staff

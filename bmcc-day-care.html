About this program

BMCC Day Care is a newly launched childcare initiative run by T.A.G Beacon Mission Christian Center, a church based in Malimbe Township, Nyamagana, Mwanza. It exists to give working parents in the surrounding community a safe, nurturing place for their children during the day — easing the pressure of balancing work and childcare. As a recently opened program, volunteers play a real part in helping it get established alongside BMCC's own team.

  • Provide safe, supervised care and play for young children
  • Support daily routines, feeding times and nap schedules
  • Assist with early learning activities, songs and storytelling
  • Help the BMCC team welcome working parents and build trust in the community
  • Always work alongside BMCC's own local staff and caregivers

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need previous experience? +

Most volunteers are welcome regardless of experience level — we match you with a placement that fits your skills. A background in childcare, education or social work is useful here but not required.

What is your child safeguarding policy? +

Every volunteer on this program is briefed on our child protection code of conduct before starting. This covers safe, appropriate interaction with children at all times, a strict no-photography-without-consent rule, and a direct line to our local staff to report any concern. Our permanent local team is present at the placement site throughout your stay.

How long can I volunteer? +

We accept volunteers for as little as 1 week up to 24 weeks (about 6 months). The minimum recommended stay for meaningful impact on this program is 2–4 weeks.

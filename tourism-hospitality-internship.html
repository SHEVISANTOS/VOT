Tourism & Hospitality Internship

About this program

Work inside small hotels, guesthouses and tour desks in Mwanza and learn how tourism really works on the ground in Tanzania.

Day to day, you'll greet and check in guests, help at reception, support restaurant and housekeeping teams, and assist tour operators with planning itineraries, answering enquiries and preparing guests for trips. You can also help local businesses grow by improving customer service, sharing English-language skills with staff, and supporting their marketing, from listings and brochures to social media and booking pages.

The program suits students and graduates in tourism, hospitality, business or marketing, and anyone curious about the travel industry. Because Mwanza is the gateway to Lake Victoria and Serengeti National Park, you'll see the industry from both sides: working with travellers during the week, and joining VOT-organized day trips and safaris yourself at weekends.

What you'll do

  • Front-office, reception & guest check-in at local hotels and guesthouses
  • Restaurant, housekeeping & guest-operations work alongside local staff
  • Tour desk work: itinerary planning, enquiries & trip preparation
  • Marketing for small tourism businesses: listings, brochures & social media
  • Suited to tourism, hospitality, business & marketing students and graduates
  • Flexible 1–24 week placements, with optional weekend tours & safaris

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need previous experience? +

Most programs welcome volunteers regardless of experience level. For healthcare placements, some medical background is preferred. We match you with a placement that fits your skills.

What are the daily working hours? +

Most placements run Monday to Friday, mornings into early afternoon. Healthcare placements can offer longer or shift-based hours if you want more hands-on time. We confirm your exact schedule with the placement before you arrive.

Can I combine this internship with tours or a safari? +

Absolutely — many volunteers add on a safari, a Zanzibar beach trip, or local Mwanza activities before or after their placement. Let us know your interest and our team can help you plan it.

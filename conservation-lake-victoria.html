About this program

This placement is built around practical, visible environmental work on and around Lake Victoria — shoreline and beach clean-ups, tree planting in community plots, and awareness campaigns run with local schools and community groups on waste, water quality and conservation. It suits volunteers on shorter stays well, and mixes easily with another placement if you're volunteering for longer. Many volunteers pair it with a weekend trip to Saanane Island National Park — Tanzania's only island national park, a short boat ride from Mwanza on Lake Victoria.

  • Lake Victoria shoreline and beach clean-ups
  • Tree planting in community plots
  • Conservation awareness campaigns with local schools and groups
  • Flexible — combines well with another program for longer stays
  • Optional weekend add-on to Saanane Island National Park

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need environmental or conservation experience? +

No. This placement welcomes volunteers of any background — a genuine interest in the environment and a willingness to do hands-on outdoor work is all you need. Environmental science, geography and conservation students are especially well suited.

What should I bring or wear? +

Closed shoes, clothes you don't mind getting dirty, sun protection and a reusable water bottle. We'll brief you on the day's activity and any specific gear needed before you head out.

Can I combine this with another placement? +

Yes — this program mixes well with another placement such as Education or Community Development, especially if you're volunteering for several weeks. Tell us your interests when you apply and we'll help build your schedule.

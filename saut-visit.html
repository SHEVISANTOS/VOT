About this excursion

St. Augustine University of Tanzania (SAUT) is the largest private university and third largest university overall in Tanzania, located just 3 km from VOT Hostel. Founded by Catholic Bishops in 1998 and accredited in 2002, SAUT extends over 600 acres in the Nyegezi-Malimbe area, 10 km south of Mwanza on the shores of Lake Victoria, and is home to over 10,000 students from Tanzania and abroad.

  • Founded by Catholic Bishops in 1998, accredited in 2002
  • Over 10,000 students, extending over 600 acres in Nyegezi-Malimbe
  • Main Campus — Administration & Faculty of Business Administration
  • Malimbe Campus — Law, Social Sciences, Engineering, Education & Mass Communication
  • International student community from Kenya, Uganda, Sudan, Ethiopia, Burundi, Malawi, Zambia, Germany and other nations
  • Just 3 km from VOT Hostel — an easy, low-key excursion on any free day

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need to book this in advance? +

Let the VOT team know a few days ahead so we can confirm a good time on campus, but given SAUT is just 3 km away this visit can often be arranged at short notice too.

What should I bring or wear? +

Smart-casual clothing is appreciated on campus. Bring a notebook or camera if you'd like — many volunteers enjoy chatting with SAUT's international student community.

Can I visit if I'm in Mwanza on a short trip, not a full placement? +

Yes — this excursion is open to current volunteers and short-term visitors alike. Contact us and we'll arrange the visit around your dates.

About this program

This internship is for engineering students who want practical, on-the-ground field experience rather than a classroom-only placement. You'll support community infrastructure and facilities projects alongside local partners in and around Mwanza, working directly with local partner project staff.

Because this placement is on request rather than a fixed program, it's matched to whatever partner project needs are current at the time you apply. We ask volunteers to share their background and CV when applying, so we can pair you with a project and supervision that fits your experience.

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Is this placement available year-round? +

This program is on request rather than a fixed, always-running placement. We match volunteers to current partner project needs, so exact tasks and availability are confirmed once you apply.

Do I need a specific engineering discipline? +

No single discipline is required. Share your background and CV when you apply, and we'll match you with a project and local partner suited to your studies and experience.

Can I combine this with another placement? +

Yes — this program can mix with another placement such as Community or Environment, especially on a longer stay. Tell us your interests and background when you apply and we'll help build your schedule.

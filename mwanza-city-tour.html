About this program

Experience the vibrant city of Mwanza with arrangements made by VOT management. This half-day or full-day excursion takes you through the city's landmarks and everyday life — a relaxed way to spend free time during your placement or a short visit as a tourist.

  • Capri Point Peninsula — immerse yourself in the city's rich history influenced by German, British and Indian cultures
  • Bismark Rock — iconic landmark perched atop boulders by the Kamanga ferry harbor
  • Rock City Shopping Mall — Tanzania's largest mall with cinema, casino, restaurants and supermarkets
  • Boat cruise activities — available on the shores of Lake Victoria (extra charge)
  • International dining — visit popular Chinese and Indian restaurants

Additional sites

  • CCM Kirumba and Nyamagana Stadium (football fans)
  • The Hanging Tree in city center
  • Mwaloni Fish Market (Eastern & Central Africa's largest)
  • Mlango Mmoja & Makorobi (shopping)
  • Germany Gunzet House & Indian old buildings
  • Maasai Market
  • Railway Station
  • Bugando Hospital

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Who can join this tour? +

Anyone — current VOT volunteers with free time, or visitors on a short trip to Mwanza. All ages are welcome, and the tour is arranged directly with VOT management.

What should I bring? +

Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a camera and some spending money for shopping, dining or the optional boat cruise on Lake Victoria.

Can I combine this with a volunteer placement? +

Yes — this is a popular free-time excursion for volunteers on any program. Tell us when you'd like to go and we'll help arrange it around your placement schedule.

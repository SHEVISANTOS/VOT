About this excursion

The Sukuma Museum is a community-oriented organisation committed to promoting and honouring the traditional and contemporary arts of the Sukuma people, Tanzania's largest ethnic group. On this half-day trip from VOT Hostel, Sukuma elders share traditional history and arts with younger generations in an interactive, educational environment — a rich cultural add-on for volunteers on any placement.

  • Traditional Sukuma dwellings
  • Grass house of a traditional healer
  • Blacksmith's tools
  • Rotating cylinder illustrating Sukuma counting words (1–10)
  • Artifacts from ancient Sukuma chiefdoms and dance societies
  • Home of the famous Bulabo Dance Festival in June, where dancers compete using various animals as props
  • Optional workshops offering training in traditional Sukuma arts

Good to Know

Requirements & what's included

Do I need to book this in advance? +

We recommend telling us a few days ahead so we can arrange transport and a guide, but the trip can often be added to your schedule at short notice if you're already volunteering with us — just ask the VOT team.

What should I bring or wear? +

Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection and a camera. Modest dress is appreciated when visiting the museum's traditional dwellings and meeting Sukuma elders.

Can I visit if I'm in Mwanza on a short trip, not a full placement? +

Yes — this excursion is open to current volunteers and short-term visitors alike. Contact us and we'll arrange the visit around your dates.
